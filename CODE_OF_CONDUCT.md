# Code of Conduct

This project follows principles equivalent to the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good faith and focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions about security to private channels until a fix is available.
- Refrain from harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing other people's private information.
- Use this project only for authorized security work. Do not contribute features intended to scan systems without permission.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow these expectations, and may restrict participants who repeatedly violate them.

Report conduct concerns privately through the same GitHub channel described in SECURITY.md. Reports are reviewed by the maintainer, who will decide on a response and communicate it to the reporter.
